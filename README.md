# Building Cost Forecast

A single-page planning tool: add the expenses of running a building and see the total and a ranked breakdown over 1 to 72 months. Keep several scenarios and switch between them.

- Everything is saved in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing site data erases it.
- All amounts are ones you enter. The "expenses to expect" panel summarizes published guidance and names its sources; many figures come from vendors, so treat them as starting points.
- Planning estimates only. Not financial, tax or legal advice.
